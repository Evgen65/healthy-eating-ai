# Engineering rules

1. Work only on the requested milestone.
2. Do not add functionality not requested by the specification.
3. Prefer simple readable solutions.
4. Use TypeScript.
5. Keep business logic outside React components.
6. Never call external AI services directly from the frontend.
7. Future AI integrations must go through the backend.
8. Do not introduce dependencies without a clear reason.
9. Explain significant architecture changes before making them.
10. Preserve testability of the application.
